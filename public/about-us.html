Our Story

A simple search turned into a bigger idea.

At Natrio Organics, our story started the same way many good stories do — with frustration, endless searching, and a little bit of "there has to be something better than this."

Finding pure hair oils in Lahore was surprisingly difficult. Every bottle claimed to be "natural" or "organic," but once you tried them, it was a different story. Heavy fragrances, strange textures, and oils that felt more like kitchen leftovers than something you'd trust on your hair.

When we shared this struggle with friends and family, we realized it wasn't just a Lahore problem. It was the same story across Karachi, Islamabad, and all around Pakistan. Pure oils were hard to find, and many products in the market were diluted with low-quality mixtures and cheap cooking oils — while customers were paying for "premium" care.

That's when a simple search turned into a bigger idea.

Coming from a farming family, seeds were never the problem. We grew up around nature, crops, and traditional oil extraction knowledge. The ingredients were already there — pure, natural, and trusted for generations. The real challenge was figuring out how to extract these oils properly, preserve their purity, and bring them to people who were tired of compromising.

So, we decided to do it ourselves.

What started as making oils for our own homes slowly became Natrio Organics — a brand built on honesty, purity, and the belief that self-care should never come with hidden ingredients.

Today, every bottle we create is rooted in the same mission: to make natural, cold-pressed oils accessible to people across Pakistan without them having to go through the same frustrating search we did.
